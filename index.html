RootToGo Coming soon to iPhone

Trade cuttings, not cash.

RootToGo is a plant-swapping app for your neighborhood. Share your extra cuttings, pick up something new from a grower nearby, and watch each plant's family spread from home to home.

Get early access Email us and we'll invite you to the iPhone beta.

How it grows

Every swap plants something new, down here where the roots spread.

  1. List what you've grown

    Too many pothos? Snap a photo, pick its stage, and put it up for swap, as a trade or a gift.

  2. Swap with someone nearby

    Browse plants by distance, never by address. Chat in the app and meet somewhere public.

  3. Watch it travel

    Every plant keeps its family tree, so you can see how far your cutting's grandchildren have gone.

No money, ever. Give a plant away and earn Seeds to spend on your next one.
